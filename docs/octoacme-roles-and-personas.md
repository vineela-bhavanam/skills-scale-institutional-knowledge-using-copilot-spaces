# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers align delivery capacity, staffing, and technical execution with the roadmap to help teams deliver consistently and sustainably.

### Responsibilities
- Support team planning, staffing, and workload balancing
- Partner with Project Managers and Product Managers to translate priorities into execution plans
- Remove delivery blockers and coordinate dependencies across teams
- Maintain engineering health, quality, and development velocity
- Support career development, mentoring, and team operating rhythm

### Goals
- Deliver predictable execution and team performance
- Improve throughput without sacrificing quality or sustainability
- Ensure engineering work is aligned with business priorities and delivery commitments

### Typical Communication
- Team planning and staffing discussions
- Delivery status with PMs and stakeholders
- Technical trade-off conversations with product and leadership stakeholders

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Project Managers on schedules, capacity, and risk management
- Partners with Product Managers to prioritize outcomes and sequence delivery
- Supports Developers by clarifying technical constraints and enabling execution
- Escalates cross-team dependencies or resourcing issues to leadership when necessary

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects guide design decisions, system trade-offs, and technical standards to keep solutions scalable, maintainable, and aligned with project goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define or refine technical direction and architecture for the initiative
- Review major design decisions and technical trade-offs
- Ensure solutions align with quality, security, and scalability requirements
- Guide implementation patterns and technical debt decisions
- Support Developers during complex design or integration work

### Goals
- Reduce rework caused by weak technical foundations
- Keep the solution coherent, testable, and maintainable
- Support delivery without compromising long-term system health

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Technical decision records and implementation guidance
- Risk and dependency reviews with PMs and Engineering Managers

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to translate requirements into feasible technical approaches
- Provides technical guidance to Developers and supports their implementation choices
- Coordinates with Security and Compliance Leads on control and risk requirements
- Helps Project Managers understand technical dependencies and delivery risks

---

## UX Researchers / Designers

### Role Summary
UX Researchers and Designers validate user needs, shape user experience, and ensure product work is usable, accessible, and aligned with customer expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and synthesize findings into actionable insights
- Translate customer pain points into product requirements and design opportunities
- Validate usability, accessibility, and end-to-end workflows
- Support iteration based on feedback and qualitative evidence
- Partner with Product Managers and Developers on user-centered delivery

### Goals
- Improve customer adoption, satisfaction, and usability
- Reduce friction in product experiences and critical workflows
- Ensure user needs are represented in product decisions

### Typical Communication
- User research summaries and design feedback
- Product discovery workshops and usability testing sessions
- Cross-functional planning with PMs, developers, and stakeholders

### Interaction with existing roles
- Shares customer insights with Product Managers to inform prioritization
- Collaborates with Developers during implementation to validate interaction patterns
- Provides design inputs that support acceptance criteria and QA validation
- Helps Project Managers understand user and workflow risks that may affect delivery

---

## Security & Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads identify and manage security, privacy, and policy requirements to reduce risk throughout planning, development, and release.

### Responsibilities
- Assess security and compliance risks for features, integrations, and releases
- Define or validate controls, requirements, and mitigation plans
- Review architecture, data handling, and deployment practices for risk exposure
- Support incident response and access control requirements
- Ensure the team meets relevant standards and policy obligations

### Goals
- Reduce exposure to security and compliance risks
- Enable delivery without creating unacceptable operational or regulatory risk
- Support a secure, trusted customer experience

### Typical Communication
- Risk reviews and compliance checkpoints
- Security requirements in planning and release readiness discussions
- Incident escalation and post-incident follow-up

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers and Project Managers to assess how risks affect roadmap and milestones
- Partners with Technical Leads and Developers on secure design patterns and remediation
- Supports Release Managers to ensure pre-launch controls and evidence are complete
- Escalates critical issues to leadership and stakeholders when business or legal impact is significant

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness, sequencing, and communication so that changes are deployed reliably and with clear stakeholder visibility.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare release plans and deployment windows
- Confirm required quality gates, acceptance criteria, and rollback readiness
- Coordinate communication to stakeholders and support teams before and after release
- Monitor release execution and facilitate post-deploy verification
- Capture lessons learned and release-related risks for future iterations

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and improve deployment confidence
- Ensure customers and stakeholders receive clear, timely communication
- Support smooth rollout and quick recovery if issues arise

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and launch checklists
- Stakeholder notifications and support briefings
- Post-release summaries and operational follow-up

### Interaction with existing roles
- Coordinates with Project Managers on timing, dependencies, and communications
- Works with Developers and Technical Leads to confirm release readiness and rollback plans
- Partners with Security and Compliance Leads to validate controls before launch
- Aligns with Product Managers and stakeholders on communication and customer impact

---

## How these personas work together
- Project Managers and Engineering Managers align delivery plans, capacity, and risk management.
- Product Managers collaborate with UX Researchers and Designers to ensure customer value and usability are reflected in priorities.
- Technical Leads and Developers convert product and design intent into feasible, maintainable solutions.
- Security and Compliance Leads review major risks and help protect quality, privacy, and trust.
- Release Managers coordinate launch readiness and communication across the final stages of delivery.

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles help teams clarify accountability, speed up delivery decisions, and reduce ambiguity across functional boundaries.
